## Brand & Style

This design system expresses pure Sattvic nourishment, mindful daily rituals, and organic Indian dairy heritage. Tailored for conscious modern households seeking genuine A2 dairy, cold-pressed ghee, and farm-fresh nutrition, the visual language merges the serenity of dawn yogic discipline with tactile, appetizing purity.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on **warm editorial minimalism rooted in Vedic tradition**:
- **Tone**: Serene, pure, dependable, grounded, and dignified. It rejects fast-commerce visual frenzy, artificial urgency banners, and gamified reward tokens in favor of transparent provenance, batch traceability, and quiet elegance.
- **Visual Weight**: Airy Warm White foundations, lush malai-cream surface planes, deep royal indigo ink structures, and calibrated touches of solar saffron and sanctified temple gold.
- **Sensory Touchpoints**: Subtle floral linework inspired by traditional lotus engravings, unhurried negative space, generous touch targets, and rhythmic editorial headings.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the sacred dairy cycle: pure royal indigo inks, the early morning sun over farm pastures, churned butterfat, and warm unpasteurized milk.

### Core Brand Tokens
- **Brand Navy (`#1F2A6B`)**: Primary brand identity. Evokes trust, early-morning stillness, and timeless authority. Used for primary actions, structural headings, and navigation icons.
- **Navy Dark (`#151D4D`)**: Deep shade used for pressed button states, deep footer zones, and high-emphasis brand hero blocks.
- **Navy Container (`#E6E8F5`)**: Soft indigo wash used for active tab pills, quiet notifications, and category badges.
- **Saffron (`#F47B20`)**: Radiant morning energy. Dedicated exclusively to secondary CTAs (e.g., "Subscribe Now", "Modify Delivery"), promotion badges, and live order alerts.
- **Saffron Container (`#FDEBDC`)**: Subdued warm wash used for milk subscription calendars and early-dispatch chips.
- **Gold (`#C9A03D`)**: Subtle Vedic luxury. Reserved for artisanal ghee certifications, farm-purity seals, customer review stars, and lotus flourishes.

### Neutral & Surface System
- **Warm White (`#FFFCF6`)**: Primary application canvas background, radiating natural warmth over sterile cool grays.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure elevated card surfaces, sheet layers, and interactive panels.
- **Cream (`#FBF1DC`)**: Rich milk-cream tint for featured product backdrops, dietary notes, and subscription highlights.
- **Outline / Warm Border (`#E7E2D6`)**: Muted organic border token ensuring subtle card definition without harsh demarcation.
- **Text Primary (`#1B2030`)**: Deep blue-tinted slate for effortless readability and high contrast.
- **Text Secondary (`#5E6475`)**: Soft mineral slate for batch codes, volume metrics, and farm metadata.
- **Text Disabled (`#A3A8B8`)**: Low-contrast tone for inactive actions and historical logs.

### Functional Status Tokens
- **Success (`#1F9D63`)** / **Container (`#E3F6EC`)**: Successful delivery verification, fresh batch pass, and active recurring subscription.
- **Amber / Warning (`#E8A317`)** / **Container (`#FFF4DB`)**: Pending payments, weather delays, and cutoff time warnings. **Crucial Rule**: Pending payments must strictly use Amber (`#E8A317`), never Brand Saffron.
- **Error (`#D64545`)** / **Container (`#FDE8E8`)**: Failed deliveries, card lapses, and out-of-stock notices.
- **Info (`#3B82F6`)** / **Container (`#E6F0FF`)**: Route changes, temperature-controlled transit tracking, and farmer notices.

## Typography

The typography pairing reflects classic editorial refinement alongside legible, effortless modern utility:
- **Playfair Display**: Used for high-impact hero claims, sacred brand narratives, product titles, and editorial mottos. High optical contrast and calligraphic serifs honour traditional heritage without sacrificing clarity.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans**: A clean, contemporary geometric grotesque with humanized curves. Chosen for nutritional information, doorstep delivery schedules, subscription configuration flows, and micro-labels.
- **Editorial Microcopy**: Uppercase tracking (`0.04em`) is applied strictly to `label-sm` when designating origin markers (e.g., "PURE GIR COW A2", "FARM CHURNED", "HARVEST DATE").

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an 8pt rhythmic spatial cadence anchored on an authentic, uncluttered grid:
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Focuses on full-width thumb-friendly subscription cards and bottom sheets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Accommodates two-column delivery logs and subscription planners.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout max-width constrained to `1240px` with `3rem` margins. Generous whitespace around high-resolution farm imagery, Vedic methodology narratives, and interactive purity lab certificates.
- **Component Rhythms**: Interior card padding scales consistently at `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` to provide a calm, breathable layout that never crowds product information.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes tactile paper and ceramic layers over synthetic drop shadows:
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#FFFCF6` (Warm White). Flat, unmoving, restful backdrop.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: `#FFFFFF` (Surface) resting over `#FFFCF6`, bounded by a hairline border: `1px solid #E7E2D6`. Paired with an ambient natural shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(31, 42, 107, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Hover States & Active Plan Cards)**: Elevated card state featuring `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(31, 42, 107, 0.08)` and subtle border illumination via `#C9A03D` (Gold) or `#1F2A6B` (Brand Navy).
- **Layer 3 (Floating Headers & Navigation Bars)**: Frosted organic surface using `rgba(255, 252, 246, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a bottom border `1px solid #E7E2D6`.
- **Layer 4 (Modals & Delivery Selection Sheets)**: Surface-white sheets elevated via `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(21, 29, 77, 0.16)` with a warm backdrop dim of `rgba(21, 29, 77, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The geometry draws from earthen clay matkas, milk bottles, and organic botanicals. Corners feel smooth and handcrafted rather than sharp or industrial:
- **Cards & Primary Modules**: Strict `20px` radius (`rounded-2xl`). Delivers comfortable, approachable framing for dairy packs and daily delivery slots.
- **Buttons & Text Fields**: Standardized `14px` radius. Creates an ergonomic, solid touch footprint that matches card curves harmoniously.
- **Filter Chips & Status Pills**: Full pill radius (`9999px`). Imparts a soft, organic feel for dates, dietary tags, and milk fat percentages.
- **Inner Imagery & Thumbnails**: `12px` to `16px` radius inside parent cards to preserve concentric visual padding.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Brand Navy)**: Background `#1F2A6B`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `14px`, height `48px` (mobile `52px`). Hover `#151D4D`. Focus ring `3px solid rgba(31, 42, 107, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary CTA (Solar Saffron)**: Background `#F47B20`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `14px`. Used for "Subscribe Daily", "Start Free Trial", and quick add-to-morning-basket actions. Hover `#E06A14`.
- **Tertiary / Outline**: Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #1F2A6B`, text `#1F2A6B`, radius `14px`.
- **Warm Ghost Button**: Background transparent, text `#1F2A6B`, hover background `#FBF1DC`.

### Chips & Filters
- **Default Chip**: Height `34px`, border `1px solid #E7E2D6`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#5E6475`, radius `9999px`.
- **Selected Chip**: Background `#1F2A6B`, border `1px solid #1F2A6B`, text `#FFFFFF`, subtle inner glow.
- **Dietary & Feature Badges**: Background `#FBF1DC` (Cream), border `1px solid #E7E2D6`, text `#1B2030`, with an optional Gold (`#C9A03D`) icon accent.

### Cards
- **Product & Plan Card**: Radius `20px`, surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E7E2D6`, padding `20px`. Top section houses milk fat ratios or certification pills, followed by editorial title and daily pricing units.
- **Subscription Summary Card**: Background `#FBF1DC` (Cream) with an outline in `#E7E2D6`, featuring delivery frequency (Alternate Days, Daily Dawn, Custom).

### Input Fields
- **Standard Field**: Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E7E2D6`, radius `14px`, text `#1B2030`, placeholder `#A3A8B8`.
- **Focused State**: Border `1.5px solid #1F2A6B`, outline `3px solid rgba(31, 42, 107, 0.12)`.
- **Error State**: Border `1.5px solid #D64545`, helper text in `#D64545`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox**: `20px x 20px`, radius `6px`, border `1.5px solid #E7E2D6`. When checked: fill `#1F2A6B` with white checkmark.
- **Radio Button**: `20px x 20px`, circular, border `1.5px solid #E7E2D6`. When active: outer border `#1F2A6B` containing an inner circle dot of `#1F2A6B` (`8px`).

### Lists & Navigation
- **Doorstep Delivery Timeline**: Left vertical rail in `#E7E2D6` with node dots in Brand Navy (`#1F2A6B`) for completed milestones, Saffron (`#F47B20`) for "Out for Dawn Delivery", and Amber (`#E8A317`) for reschedule requests.
- **Bottom Navigation (Mobile)**: Background `rgba(255, 252, 246, 0.95)`, blur `16px`, height `64px`, border-top `1px solid #E7E2D6`. Active icons wash with `#1F2A6B`, inactive in `#5E6475`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **A2 Purity & Lab Certificate Badge**: Circular or capsule seal in `#C9A03D` with hairline lotus linework displaying batch test results and chilling timestamps.
- **Milk Calendar Selector**: Interactive horizontal date ribbon highlighting upcoming 6:00 AM delivery drops; active slot fills `#1F2A6B` with text `#FFFFFF` and morning dew indicators.
