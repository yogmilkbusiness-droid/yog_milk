## Brand & Style

The brand identity centers on "From Nature to Nourish You" — treating pure, farm-fresh dairy not as an everyday grocery commodity, but as a grounded, sacred morning wellness ritual. It pairs the heritage, purity, and clinical freshness of Vedic milk traditions with contemporary editorial calm.

### Visual Style
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Editorial Wellness Minimalism. Calm, generous whitespace, pristine ivory layers, and disciplined typographic scale.
- **Tone:** Authoritative, pristine, tranquil, and appetizing.
- **Core Principles:**
  - **No Gamification:** Absence of scratch cards, coins, loyalty points, or wallet gimmicks. Interactions prioritize direct subscription management, transparent deliveries, and artisan single-origin integrity.
  - **Tactile Purity:** Rich deep navy anchor surfaces combined with raw farm warm cream backgrounds (`#FBF1DC`, `#FFFCF6`) to evoke natural unhomogenized milk fat and warm sunlit farm mornings.
  - **Quiet Luxury:** Restrained touches of warm saffron and aged butter gold denote farm batch certifications, cold-chain telemetry, and editorial distinction.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sensation of calm nourishment. Rather than sterile tech blues, the system anchors itself in deep night-harvest navy, tempered by dairy cream whites and sun-touched warm accents.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary Anchor (`#151D4D` & `#1F2A6B`):** Brand Navy represents structural trust, dairy bottle caps, key actions, primary typography, and nav anchors. 
- **Warm Canvas Neutral (`#FFFCF6` & `#FFFFFF`):** High-emulsion warm ivory base and pristine porcelain cards. Backgrounds never use sterile neutral gray `#FFFFFF` on full-bleed containers.
- **Tonal Milk Cream (`#FBF1DC`):** Used across high-touch callout panels, farm origin statements, and milk fat composition metrics.
- **Saffron Glow (`#F47B20` & `#FDEBDC` container):** The dawn accent. Reserved strictly for subscription pause/resume, early morning delivery slots, secondary highlights, and dawn reminders.
- **Vedic Gold (`#C9A03D`):** Editorial seal of purity, lab test batch ribbons, A2 beta-casein verification badges, and star ratings.
- **Outlines & Dividers (`#E7E2D6`):** Gentle dairy-creme borders replacing cold modern grays.

### System & State Tokens
- **Payment Pending:** Amber `#E8A317` with `#FFF4DB` container.
- **Delivery Verified / Active:** Success Green `#1F9D63` with `#E3F6EC` container.
- **Alert / Cold Chain Disruption:** Error Red `#D64545` with `#FDE8E8` container.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial balance: the high-contrast transitional serif **Playfair Display** conveys artisanal lineage and wellness pedigree, while **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides clear, rhythmic readability for milk schedules, nutritional panels, and invoice breakdowns.

### Application Guidelines
- **Editorial Voice (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for product story headings, farm source locations, certification mastheads, and catalog categories (e.g., *“Raw A2 Gir Cow Milk”*, *“Bilona Cultured Ghee”*). It must never be used below 20px.
- **Operational Clarity (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Standardizes all quantitative information—delivery slots (e.g., *“05:30 AM – 07:00 AM”*), subscription frequencies, bill details, and batch timestamps.
- **Tracking:** Micro badges and category caps employ deliberate positive letter spacing (`0.06em`) to ensure legibility on soft cream card containers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces generous negative space, mirroring the tranquil nature of dawn farm routines. It uses a structured grid that adapts gracefully across phone viewports (daily schedule checks) to desktop screens (subscription accounts and farm stories).

### Grid Structure
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px outer margin, 16px gutter. Content cards extend full-width or side-scroll in tactile horizontal shelf lists.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns, 24px outer margin, 20px gutter.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max-width 1280px centered container, 48px outer margin, 24px gutter.

### Rhythm & Composition
- Structural containers use internal padding of `space-lg` (24px) to keep bottle photography and nutritional parameters distinct.
- Tight list pairings (e.g., delivery date next to item count) rely on `space-sm` (8px). Section modules maintain `space-xl` (36px) gaps to prevent sensory clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids harsh tech shadows or deep industrial black cast-shadows. Instead, elevation uses warm, low-intensity diffused amber-navy shadows that mimic early morning dawn light bouncing off white porcelain and milk glass.

### Depth Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat surface tinted in Warm White (`#FFFCF6`).
- **Tier 1 (Catalog & Content Cards):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a warm, low-opacity drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(21, 29, 77, 0.04)`, enclosed by a thin 1px border of `#E7E2D6`.
- **Tier 2 (Sticky Delivery Bar & Subscription Drawers):** `0 10px 30px -4px rgba(21, 29, 77, 0.08)`, creating separation for daily schedule updates and basket summaries.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Modal & Pause Schedule Selector):** `0 20px 40px -6px rgba(21, 29, 77, 0.12)`, backed by an ivory backdrop-blur: `rgba(255, 252, 246, 0.8)` with an 8px blur radius.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture balances gentle curvature with organic form. Strict mathematical roundedness level `2` anchors the structural rhythm.

### Geometric Rules
- **Product & Content Cards:** Fixed at `20px` radius, giving dairy bottles, artisan curd tubs, and glass bottles an organic, tactile framing.
- **Interactive Buttons & Controls:** Standardized at `14px` radius, offering a softened handheld touch-point that feels calm rather than sharp.
- **Status Badges & Chips:** Full pill shape (`9999px`) across all states to preserve immediate scanability and gentle contouring.
- **Border Inset:** All cards use a structural `1px` border of `#E7E2D6` to preserve edge definition against both `#FFFCF6` and `#FBF1DC` tiers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Subscribe / Confirm Morning Run):** Navy Dark (`#151D4D`) background with white typography (`#FFFFFF`). 14px border radius, 48px height, medium Plus Jakarta Sans label. No inner glow. Hover subtle lift with `rgba(21, 29, 77, 0.9)`.
- **Secondary CTA (Edit Quantity / Pause):** Saffron outline or Saffron Container (`#FDEBDC`) with Saffron text (`#F47B20`). 14px border radius.
- **Subtle Action:** Pure Warm White (`#FFFCF6`) button with 1px border (`#E7E2D6`) and Navy text.

### Badges & Status Indicators
The system defines 3 distinct, non-conflicting pill models:
1. **Order Status Badge (Soft Filled Pill):**
   - *Active / Scheduled:* `#E3F6EC` background, `#1F9D63` label, dot prefix.
   - *Paused:* `#FFF4DB` background, `#E8A317` label.
   - *Dispatched:* `#E6E8F5` background, `#1F2A6B` label.
2. **Payment Status Badge (Outlined Pill with ₹ icon):**
   - Thin 1px outline with soft tint fill. Examples: Paid (`#1F9D63` outline, `#E3F6EC` fill with `₹`), Pending (`#E8A317` outline, `#FFF4DB` fill with `₹`).
3. **Delivery Slot Badge (Soft Pill with Truck Icon):**
   - Always paired with an inline micro truck icon. Tinted container `#E6E8F5` with `#151D4D` text displaying exact slot: *“05:30 – 07:00 AM”*.

### Cards
- **Product Offering Card:** Elevated at Tier 1 with 20px radius. Displays real Indian dairy catalog attributes: fat percentage (e.g., *“4.8% Natural Fat”*), batch lab-report link, glass bottle exchange toggle, quantity selector, and Playfair Display title.
- **Daily Calendar Delivery Card:** Horizontal card grouping date, morning door-delivery status, and subscription hold button.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** 48px height, 14px radius, warm surface background (`#FFFFFF`), border `#E7E2D6`. On active focus: border transitions to Brand Navy (`#1F2A6B`) with 0 0 0 2px `#E6E8F5`.
- **Quantity Switchers (+ / -):** Contained in a single 14px soft-pill outline with Navy glyphs and bold numerical count.

### Invoice & Billing Layout (Non-GST Direct Farmer / Consumer Bill)
- Clean, ledger-style typography without industrial tax tables. Clear breakdown of pure milk volume, per-liter price, dispatch count, bottle return credit verification, and total balance due. Outlined with `#E7E2D6` on a Cream container (`#FBF1DC`).
